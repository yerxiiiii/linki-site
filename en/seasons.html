Sharing the same seasons with you

When you come back to your desk, it's living in the very same moment—the same morning, the same rain, the same season.

Linki maps the changes of time, weather and season into the world inside its screen. Mornings have their morning light, rainy days rain inside too, and as the seasons turn the character's little routine quietly follows.

Low-interruption sensing. It senses your presence without asking for a reply. While you focus, it keeps living its own life; when you look up, there's just a small change waiting to be noticed.

Nothing to set up, nothing to check on. Spend the same hours, share the same seasons—company simply happens.
