03 / Every touch

No two touches are alike

A touch isn't a command but a light conversation—you never quite know how it will respond this time.

Tap the screen and the character responds with random events and subtle motions. It might lean in for a closer look, lazily roll over, or do nothing at all—just like a little life with a mind of its own.

Responses with no fixed script. No canned animations, no progress bars, no rules to learn. Every glance up and every touch might reveal a different detail.

It's exactly that hint of the unknown that keeps each little interaction feeling just fresh enough.
